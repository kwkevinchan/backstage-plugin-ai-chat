The session list can be searched by title, and a session can be pinned, renamed and archived. Archived sessions stay out of the list until you ask to see them, and you can restore one. After the first round of a conversation ends normally, the session gets a short title from the same model. A title you set yourself is kept. If a title cannot be made, the conversation still ends and the list still works.
